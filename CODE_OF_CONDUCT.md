# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive environment for all contributors and participants, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that creates a positive environment include:
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:
- Harassing or discriminatory comments or actions
- Publishing others' private information without consent
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Community leaders are responsible for enforcing this code of conduct. Violations can be reported by contacting the project maintainers.

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated fairly.
